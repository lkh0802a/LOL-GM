# Phase 12 — Facilities and finance

Phase 12 is an incremental completion pass on Item 7's existing functional training, analysis, recovery and youth infrastructure and the regional economic engine. The underlying world engine and domestic/international competition policies are retained.

## Scope and ownership

- `development.js`: board-funded infrastructure now has a construction schedule (18 + 12 × current tier days). The board deducts investment cash immediately, but the facility's development/analysis/recovery benefit and upkeep tier do **not** increase until a world game day reaches the delivery date. Projects reject duplicate site construction and attempts without sufficient cash. They persist as optional `team.facilityProjects` data; legacy `upgradeFacility(db,team,key)` still offers the synchronous domain command used by existing Item-7 regression probes, while seasonal board automation explicitly selects deferred construction.
- `season.js`: completed construction activates once on the next processed world date. The UI's office/staff panel displays facility tiers, upkeep and outstanding completion dates. This preserves the existing club-board investment policy and avoids forcing the manager to click repetitive infrastructure upgrades.
- `finance.js`: `team.finance.prepaid` captures already-settled facility capex, signing bonuses, transfer fees paid/received and staff severance. The annual P&L now displays these categories, but its cash posting subtracts/reverses already-settled items to prevent double-charging. The pending ledger clears at close and reappears on saves as needed. Historic saves without this optional field are accepted.
- `financeForecast(db,team)`: a conservative, itemized expected income/expense/net/cash outlook from current league interest, fanbase, owner, valid sponsor, known current-season wins, payroll, facility/staff/operations upkeep, spending tax and committed transactions. Uncertain prizes, future victories, uncommitted deals, contingent bonuses and tax redistribution are excluded. A *forecast* is not an official season financial statement and may differ from the final result.
- `ui-manager.js` and `ui-season.js`: on the history tab, players can compare a club forecast to the exact previous season's confirmed revenue, expense and net, plus the SFR top-five cap amount/percentage where applicable. The regional club cash table remains.
- `offseason.js`: the shared infrastructure investment heuristic now requires *both* current liquidity and positive estimated year-end cash above the philosophy-specific reserve; the algorithm is the same for player-controlled and AI boards and respects different regional pay scales.

## Invariants and acceptance

- World save schema **v15**, compact format **2**, historical save migrators and previous league rules remain unchanged.
- No existing match, draft, patch or signing choices are delegated differently.
- `scripts/finance-acceptance.mjs` validates construction lead time, no early buffs, duplicate/insufficient-fund protection, old/new save round-trip, ledger integrity, prepaid-once closeout reconciliation, forecast, UI visibility and board reserve guard.
- `npm run check` also runs the previously established 01–11m regressions, world smoke and two-season career acceptance. `npm run perf` and `npm run build` remain mandatory gates, with post-merge CI and generated standalone parity verified separately.

## Known boundaries

Existing instant `upgradeFacility(db,t,key)` remains supported for old engine callers/test fixtures; the automated board path uses timed delivery. A construction's clock is checked at scheduled world-day progression, so if no day advances, the project is shown as pending even past a calendar date. The forecast is deliberately bounded rather than projecting hypothetical tournament winnings or deals. Full long-horizon 100-season economy tuning remains under the separate long-run QA stage; this phase does not claim that simulation as completed.

## 12-B — World simulation depth beyond the first pass

The first 12 pass primarily audited prepaid capex/cash and added a static-looking forecast. This second pass attaches financial consequences to team history, the club's operating model and its sporting strategy:

- **Commercial demand**: broadcast revenue now responds to the region's hype and each club's fan reach. Merchandising responds to supporter base and player star appeal; overseas tournament appearances incur operating travel costs. Income and expenses are produced by the same shared functions for both forecasts and closeouts, so UI budget outlook and annual ledgers use the same base mechanics.
- **Sponsor market**: an annual board/manager can choose a one-year guaranteed agreement, a bonus-heavy one-year contract (paid per domestic win and on an actual named domestic milestone), or a lower-guarantee three-year agreement. All are priced from actual fan/reputation/league market data instead of a fixed label. Annual settlement independently verifies domestic final/playoff/survival targets; forecasts **exclude** the conditional milestone payout until earned. AI clubs choose offers according to projected terms, ambition and liquidity without overriding active agreements. Existing sponsor saves retain their agreed terms.
- **Financial stress**: cash coverage of forecast operating costs produces a board runway/risk category and influences available renewal/signing budget, discretionary AI staff spending, transfer acquisitions and facility projects. In contrast, budget stress does not automatically strip human managers of strategic contract decisions. Severe multi-year losses still permit ownership changes, but recapitalization is explicitly tracked as *capital* outside operating profit.
- **Reserve teams**: a cash-negative reserve receives an explicit transfer from its own parent, booked as `academyFunding` at the reserve and `academySupport` at the parent. Parent and reserve rows reconcile with actual cash; money is not created by silently resetting the reserve's deficit.
- **Specialized infrastructure**: training grows the first team, youth infrastructure has an age-weighted effect for prospects and improves player development appeal, analysis improves existing strategic preparation, recovery restores daily condition, and a fifth dedicated **scouting** facility improves actual observation gain. Board upgrade selection now weighs the current roster's ages, club philosophy, diminishing returns, cost, liquidity and cash forecast, rather than a flat yearly coin flip. Existing four-facility saves gain a default level-one scouting unit on restoration and retain schema v15 / save format 2.
- **Real expense trails**: manual scouting expenditures now enter the same already-paid ledger; operating liabilities include international travel and shortfall interest, and yearly sponsor milestones are reported separately. The UI explains expected vs guaranteed payouts, five facility effects, current runway, and previous capital injections without presenting uncertain bonuses as cash.
- `scripts/finance-depth-acceptance.mjs` verifies market dynamics, earned/unearned sponsor incentives, insolvency payroll discipline, specialized youth/scouting facilities, parent–reserve balance conservation, historical save compatibility, and UI access. It runs after the original Phase-12 accounting and construction acceptance as part of `npm run check`.

These enrichments deliberately preserve player agency: strategic recruitment, offers and sponsor selection for the managed club are not auto-committed, while club-board administration and AI club decisions continue to use shared policies. Full long-run 100-season inflation/competitive balance remains a separately scheduled QA exercise; completing this vertical slice must not be described as completing that long-run validation.
